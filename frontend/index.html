Welcome to our Coffee Shop

Brewed with care since day one.

CafeBot started with a simple idea: serve the coffee we love to drink, in a place people want to stay. We source beans from small farms, roast in small batches, and bake everything in-house each morning.

Whether you need a quick espresso on the way to work or a quiet corner for the afternoon, we have a cup with your name on it.
